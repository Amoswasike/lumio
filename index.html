WHAT IS LUMIO?

A clearer way to manage everyday finances.

Lumio Finance is a personal finance management application designed to help you understand, organize, and manage your money in one place.

It brings everyday financial activities such as income, expenses, budgets, savings goals, bills, notifications, and financial analytics into a single workspace.

Instead of looking at individual financial activities in isolation, Lumio helps connect them so you can see the bigger picture of your everyday finances.

V1 TRANSPARENCY

Personal finance management — not a bank.

Lumio V1 is a personal finance management application. It is not a bank, investment platform, or financial institution.

The current V1 architecture uses browser-based local storage for application data. Users should therefore protect their credentials and the devices they use to access Lumio.
