LUMIO FEATURES

Everything you need to manage your everyday finances.

Lumio brings income, expenses, budgets, savings goals, bills, notifications, search, and financial analytics into one clear, organized workspace.

ONE FINANCIAL WORKSPACE

Track. Plan. Understand.

Lumio connects the everyday parts of personal finance so you can see where your money goes, plan where it should go, and better understand your financial position.

↗

Transactions

Record income and expenses with amounts, dates, categories, descriptions, and notes. Keep your everyday financial activity organized in one place.

Your transaction records provide the foundation for understanding spending, income, cash flow, and budget activity.

◫

Budgets

Create budgets for areas such as housing, food, transport, utilities, shopping, and healthcare.

Track the budgeted amount, spending, remaining amount, and utilization while keeping budget activity connected to relevant transactions.

◎

Financial goals

Create savings goals with target amounts and monitor your progress toward the financial outcomes you are working to achieve.

Keep longer-term objectives visible alongside your everyday financial activity.

◷

Bills

Keep recurring and upcoming bills organized with amounts, categories, due dates, and payment status.

Manage bills alongside your budgets, goals, and transactions so upcoming obligations remain part of your overall financial picture.

⌁

Financial analytics

Go beyond individual transactions with financial summaries and analytics designed to help you understand your money.

Review cash flow, income composition, expense composition, savings trends, and budget performance.

!

Notifications

Stay informed about relevant financial events and application activity through a centralized notification experience.

Notification preferences help you control how application information is presented to you.

⌕

Global search

Search across your financial records without having to navigate through each feature area individually.

Global search supports transactions, budgets, bills, and financial goals.

◌

Personalization

Configure your Lumio experience through account and application settings.

Manage your currency, timezone, notification preferences, and theme, including dark theme support.
