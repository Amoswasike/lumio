HOW IT WORKS

A simple workflow for a clearer financial picture.

Lumio brings everyday financial activity into one workspace so you can track what is happening, plan what comes next, and understand your financial position.

01

Create your account

Start by creating your Lumio account and setting up your application preferences.

02

Add your income

Record the income you want to track, including relevant amounts, dates, categories, descriptions, and notes.

03

Record your expenses

Add your spending activity and organize it using categories such as food, transport, housing, utilities, shopping, or healthcare.

04

Create budgets

Set budgeted amounts for the areas that matter to you and monitor spending, remaining amounts, and budget utilization.

05

Add financial goals

Define savings goals with target amounts and monitor your progress toward the outcomes you are working to achieve.

06

Add your bills

Keep recurring and upcoming bills organized with amounts, categories, due dates, and payment status.

07

Review your financial picture

Use the dashboard and analytics to review income, expenses, balance, savings, savings rate, averages, largest entries, cash flow, and budget performance.

THE LUMIO WORKFLOW

Track → Plan → Understand

Lumio is built around a simple idea: understand where your money goes, plan where it should go, and use your financial information to make better-informed decisions.

01

Track

Record income and expenses and keep your everyday financial activity organized in one place.

Transactions give you the underlying information needed to understand your financial activity.

02

Plan

Use budgets, bills, and financial goals to give your money a more deliberate direction.

Keep spending priorities, upcoming obligations, and longer-term targets visible together.

03

Understand

Review your dashboard and analytics to understand the patterns within your financial activity.

Explore cash flow, income and expense composition, savings trends, and budget performance.

ONE CONNECTED WORKSPACE

Your financial information stays connected.

Transactions provide the underlying financial activity. Budgets help you plan spending. Bills keep upcoming obligations visible. Financial goals keep longer-term targets in view.

The dashboard and analytics then bring this information together into a clearer view of your financial position.
